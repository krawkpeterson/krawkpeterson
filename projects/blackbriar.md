# Blackbriar

![Blackbriar settlement in the fog](../assets/work/blackbriar.jpg)

The hollow keeps what it grows.

Procedural overgrown survival horror. Seeded terrain, settlements, roads, weather, and a world that rebuilds from a seed. Enter first person, cut the light, and walk the mud until the fog takes the houses back.

Godot 4. Forward+. Random generated terrain. Be scared. Survive.
